Errivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · Package com.errivo2026.errivo

Short version

Errivo collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account system. Everything you paste, analyze, save, or configure stays on your device. Errivo cannot read your data, because it has no permission or mechanism to transmit it.

What Errivo does not do

  • Errivo requests no Android permissions. Its manifest declares no uses-permission entries at all, including no internet access.
  • Errivo contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs.
  • Errivo has no account, login, server, or backend. There is no service to sign in to.
  • Errivo makes no network requests of any kind. It is fully functional with the device in airplane mode.
  • Errivo does not participate in platform backup. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are both disabled, so your saved analyses and settings are never copied off the device by the operating system.

What stays on your device

Errivo stores two categories of data, both locally:

Saved analysesLog text you chose to save, along with the detected pattern, root cause lines, suggestions, severity, and timestamp. Stored in a private on-device database (Room / SQLite) inside the app's sandbox.
SettingsYour theme choice (system, light, dark), language choice (system, English, Arabic), and whether analyses are saved to history. Stored in private on-device preferences (DataStore).

App settings, the current screen, and text you are actively editing are held in memory only and are not written to storage unless you explicitly save an analysis.

Log content may be sensitive

Logs frequently contain file paths, class names, device identifiers, hostnames, and sometimes fragments of application data. Errivo stores whatever you paste, so you should avoid pasting secrets, credentials, or personal data into a log. Saved analyses are protected by the operating system's normal app sandbox and app-lock mechanisms, but they are not separately encrypted by Errivo.

How to delete your data

  • Delete a single saved analysis from the History tab.
  • Delete all saved analyses from the History tab.
  • Reset individual settings from the Settings tab.
  • Use Settings → Erase all app data to delete all saved analyses, all settings, and everything else Errivo stores.
  • Uninstall the app. Because Errivo stores nothing off-device, uninstalling removes everything permanently.

Deleting data cannot be undone.

Children

Errivo is a developer utility intended for adults. Because Errivo collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 or any other age.

Your rights

Errivo collects no personal information, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on request, and no data for us to sell or share. Because all data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it through the controls listed above.

Third-party components

Errivo bundles open-source libraries under their own licenses (including AndroidX, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Hilt, and Room). These libraries run entirely on-device and do not introduce analytics or network access. Their source licenses are included in the app's license notices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the corresponding app release, and the "Last updated" date above will change. A material change in data handling would be called out in the release notes.
